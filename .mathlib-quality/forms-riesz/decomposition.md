## R1 (item 1): the Jacobs crux for eigenforms

### Plain-English proof
The thesis weight is `u ↦ uᵗ = exp₃(t log₃ u)` on the `‖3‖`-principal units with expansion
`κ(cx+d) = κ(d)·Σ (t choose n)(c/d)ⁿxⁿ` [Jac03 p. 29: "In all cases of (a b; c d) that we will
calculate with, c ≡ 0 mod 9 and d ≡ 1 mod 9. Therefore cx+d = 1 + 9(c'x+d')"] — a field-generic
construction given `‖3‖ < 1`, `‖t‖ < 1` and `t` in the closure of `ℕ` (the binomial theorem's
hypothesis).  Over `L ⊇ K₃` (isometric `ι`) the same formula defines the weight at `ι t`; the
columns are the `ι`-images of the `K₃`-columns (`map_unitPow`, `map_binomialCoeff`).  The
generating function `κcol·(linSeries)⁻¹·(quadSeries)⁻¹` is a field expression in the entries and
the column, so it commutes with `ι` [Jac03 Prop 2.6; fork `map_weightGenFun`], hence the weight
action's matrix coefficients, the certificate blocks (finite sums of twisted actions) and — by
`charPowerSeries_baseChange` — the determinant commute with `ι` [Buz07 Lemma 2.13].  So the
block operator of `U₃` over `L` equals the base-changed transcribed matrix `U3MatrixOp`, whose
eigenvector over `ℂ₃` (the crux) is the evaluation of an eigenform (bijectivity of evaluation over
`L`, Theorem 2.1 + Lemma 2.2 being `G`-level facts).
